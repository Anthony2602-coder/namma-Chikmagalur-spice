The Coffee Land of India

Chikmagaluru, nestled in the Western Ghats of Karnataka, is where India's coffee journey began. Our plantations benefit from rich red soil, cool climate, and abundant rainfall — creating beans with exceptional flavour profiles.

We work directly with local farmers to bring you freshly harvested Arabica and Robusta coffee, along with world-renowned Malabar black pepper.
